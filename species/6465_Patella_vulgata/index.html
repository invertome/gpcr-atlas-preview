Patella vulgata

Gastropoda, PatellogastropodaSpecies entry 518 of 593 in taxonomic orderBack to its clade in Plate 1

Patella vulgata has 269 class-A GPCRs. The median for Gastropoda, Patellogastropoda is 249 across 20 species. It ranks 8 of 20 there and 463 of 593 in the atlas. This is 16.8 per 1,000 annotated genes.

57 of its receptors end with a named receptor family, 120 with a ligand type only, and 92 with no ligand type. Figure 1 places each of its ligand types among the other Gastropoda, Patellogastropoda species, and Figure 2 shows every receptor.
