Sepiola affinis

CephalopodaSpecies entry 246 of 593 in taxonomic orderBack to its clade in Plate 1

Sepiola affinis has 194 class-A GPCRs. The median for Cephalopoda is 158.5 across 46 species. It ranks 11 of 46 there and 506 of 593 in the atlas. This is 2.6 per 1,000 annotated genes.

27 of its receptors end with a named receptor family, 104 with a ligand type only, and 63 with no ligand type. Figure 1 places each of its ligand types among the other Cephalopoda species, and Figure 2 shows every receptor.
