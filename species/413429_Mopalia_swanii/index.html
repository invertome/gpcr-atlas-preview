PolyplacophoraSpecies entry 570 of 593 in taxonomic orderBack to its clade in Plate 1

Mopalia swanii has 428 class-A GPCRs. The median for Polyplacophora is 483 across 22 species. It ranks 17 of 22 there and 349 of 593 in the atlas. This is 5.9 per 1,000 annotated genes.

68 of its receptors end with a named receptor family, 239 with a ligand type only, and 121 with no ligand type. Figure 1 places each of its ligand types among the other Polyplacophora species, and Figure 2 shows every receptor.
