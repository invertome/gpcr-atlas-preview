Octopus maya

CephalopodaSpecies entry 259 of 593 in taxonomic orderBack to its clade in Plate 1

Octopus maya has 135 class-A GPCRs. The median for Cephalopoda is 158.5 across 46 species. It ranks 31 of 46 there and 545 of 593 in the atlas. This is 3.1 per 1,000 annotated genes.

34 of its receptors end with a named receptor family, 52 with a ligand type only, and 49 with no ligand type. Figure 1 places each of its ligand types among the other Cephalopoda species, and Figure 2 shows every receptor.
