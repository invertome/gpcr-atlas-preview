Katharina tunicata

PolyplacophoraSpecies entry 566 of 593 in taxonomic orderBack to its clade in Plate 1

Katharina tunicata has 425 class-A GPCRs. The median for Polyplacophora is 483 across 22 species. It ranks 18 of 22 there and 351 of 593 in the atlas. This is 8.3 per 1,000 annotated genes.

53 of its receptors end with a named receptor family, 250 with a ligand type only, and 122 with no ligand type. Figure 1 places each of its ligand types among the other Polyplacophora species, and Figure 2 shows every receptor.
