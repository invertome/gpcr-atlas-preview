Acanthopleura granulata

PolyplacophoraSpecies entry 575 of 593 in taxonomic orderBack to its clade in Plate 1

Acanthopleura granulata has 474 class-A GPCRs. The median for Polyplacophora is 483 across 22 species. It ranks 12 of 22 there and 300 of 593 in the atlas. This is 21.1 per 1,000 annotated genes.

56 of its receptors end with a named receptor family, 219 with a ligand type only, and 199 with no ligand type. Figure 1 places each of its ligand types among the other Polyplacophora species, and Figure 2 shows every receptor.
