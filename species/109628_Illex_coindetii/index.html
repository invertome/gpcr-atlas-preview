CephalopodaSpecies entry 231 of 593 in taxonomic orderBack to its clade in Plate 1

Illex coindetii has 231 class-A GPCRs. The median for Cephalopoda is 158.5 across 46 species. It ranks 1 of 46 there and 479 of 593 in the atlas. This is 4.9 per 1,000 annotated genes.

58 of its receptors end with a named receptor family, 129 with a ligand type only, and 44 with no ligand type. Figure 1 places each of its ligand types among the other Cephalopoda species, and Figure 2 shows every receptor.
