ScaphopodaSpecies entry 582 of 593 in taxonomic orderBack to its clade in Plate 1

Antalis vulgaris has 428 class-A GPCRs. The median for Scaphopoda is 446.5 across 4 species. It ranks 3 of 4 there and 349 of 593 in the atlas. This is 6.7 per 1,000 annotated genes.

48 of its receptors end with a named receptor family, 307 with a ligand type only, and 73 with no ligand type. Figure 1 places each of its ligand types among the other Scaphopoda species, and Figure 2 shows every receptor.
