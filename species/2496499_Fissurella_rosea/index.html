Gastropoda, VetigastropodaSpecies entry 522 of 593 in taxonomic orderBack to its clade in Plate 1

54 of its receptors end with a named receptor family, 299 with a ligand type only, and 70 with no ligand type. Figure 1 places each of its ligand types among the other Gastropoda, Vetigastropoda species, and Figure 2 shows every receptor.
