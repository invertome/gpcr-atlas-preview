PolyplacophoraSpecies entry 569 of 593 in taxonomic orderBack to its clade in Plate 1

Mopalia muscosa has 462 class-A GPCRs. The median for Polyplacophora is 483 across 22 species. It ranks 15 of 22 there and 314 of 593 in the atlas. This is 8.7 per 1,000 annotated genes.

101 of its receptors end with a named receptor family, 223 with a ligand type only, and 138 with no ligand type. Figure 1 places each of its ligand types among the other Polyplacophora species, and Figure 2 shows every receptor.
