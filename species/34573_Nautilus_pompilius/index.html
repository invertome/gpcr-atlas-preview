CephalopodaSpecies entry 267 of 593 in taxonomic orderBack to its clade in Plate 1

Nautilus pompilius has 208 class-A GPCRs. The median for Cephalopoda is 158.5 across 46 species. It ranks 7 of 46 there and 495 of 593 in the atlas. This is 9.2 per 1,000 annotated genes.

33 of its receptors end with a named receptor family, 137 with a ligand type only, and 38 with no ligand type. Figure 1 places each of its ligand types among the other Cephalopoda species, and Figure 2 shows every receptor.
