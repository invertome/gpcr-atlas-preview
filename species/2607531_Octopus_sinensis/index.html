Octopus sinensis

CephalopodaSpecies entry 261 of 593 in taxonomic orderBack to its clade in Plate 1

Octopus sinensis has 196 class-A GPCRs. The median for Cephalopoda is 158.5 across 46 species. It ranks 9 of 46 there and 503 of 593 in the atlas. This is 10.4 per 1,000 annotated genes.

40 of its receptors end with a named receptor family, 124 with a ligand type only, and 32 with no ligand type. Figure 1 places each of its ligand types among the other Cephalopoda species, and Figure 2 shows every receptor.
