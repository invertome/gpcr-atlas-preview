Hapalochlaena maculosa

CephalopodaSpecies entry 254 of 593 in taxonomic orderBack to its clade in Plate 1

Hapalochlaena maculosa has 136 class-A GPCRs. The median for Cephalopoda is 158.5 across 46 species. It ranks 29 of 46 there and 543 of 593 in the atlas. This is 2.1 per 1,000 annotated genes.

37 of its receptors end with a named receptor family, 61 with a ligand type only, and 38 with no ligand type. Figure 1 places each of its ligand types among the other Cephalopoda species, and Figure 2 shows every receptor.
