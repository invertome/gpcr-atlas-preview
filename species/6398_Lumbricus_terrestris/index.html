Lumbricus terrestris

OutgroupsSpecies entry 2 of 593 in taxonomic orderBack to its clade in Plate 1

Lumbricus terrestris has 326 class-A GPCRs. The median for Outgroups is 354 across 15 species. It ranks 9 of 15 there and 427 of 593 in the atlas. This is 12.3 per 1,000 annotated genes.

146 of its receptors end with a named receptor family, 170 with a ligand type only, and 10 with no ligand type. Figure 1 places each of its ligand types among the other Outgroups species, and Figure 2 shows every receptor.
