Peltospira operculata has 325 class-A GPCRs. The median for Gastropoda, Neomphalina is 380 across 13 species. It ranks 9 of 13 there and 429 of 593 in the atlas. This is 5.9 per 1,000 annotated genes.

35 of its receptors end with a named receptor family, 232 with a ligand type only, and 58 with no ligand type. Figure 1 places each of its ligand types among the other Gastropoda, Neomphalina species, and Figure 2 shows every receptor.
