Theodoxus fluviatilis

Gastropoda, NeritimorphaSpecies entry 498 of 593 in taxonomic orderBack to its clade in Plate 1

Theodoxus fluviatilis has 223 class-A GPCRs. The median for Gastropoda, Neritimorpha is 308 across 2 species. It ranks 2 of 2 there and 485 of 593 in the atlas. This is 5.9 per 1,000 annotated genes.

23 of its receptors end with a named receptor family, 151 with a ligand type only, and 49 with no ligand type. Figure 1 places each of its ligand types among the other Gastropoda, Neritimorpha species, and Figure 2 shows every receptor.
