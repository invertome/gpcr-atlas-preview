Gastropoda, PatellogastropodaSpecies entry 499 of 593 in taxonomic orderBack to its clade in Plate 1

67 of its receptors end with a named receptor family, 144 with a ligand type only, and 81 with no ligand type. Figure 1 places each of its ligand types among the other Gastropoda, Patellogastropoda species, and Figure 2 shows every receptor.
