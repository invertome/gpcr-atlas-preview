Haliotis rubra

Gastropoda, VetigastropodaSpecies entry 529 of 593 in taxonomic orderBack to its clade in Plate 1

Haliotis rubra has 425 class-A GPCRs. The median for Gastropoda, Vetigastropoda is 475 across 41 species. It ranks 25 of 41 there and 351 of 593 in the atlas. This is 14.3 per 1,000 annotated genes.

73 of its receptors end with a named receptor family, 248 with a ligand type only, and 104 with no ligand type. Figure 1 places each of its ligand types among the other Gastropoda, Vetigastropoda species, and Figure 2 shows every receptor.
