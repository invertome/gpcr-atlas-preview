Methods

Methods for ligand type calls, receptor family calls, and the novelty threshold will be described here.

Terms used on this site

Agreement rules
The rules that combine the pocket check, the receptor's community in the embedding graph and its nearest characterized receptor into one call. A ligand type is assigned when the pocket confirms it, or when the characterized receptors in the receptor's community mainly share it, the pocket does not contradict it and, if that share is narrow, the nearest characterized receptor agrees. Otherwise the receptor is Unassigned, or Orphan community if its community holds too few characterized receptors and no rescue applies. Receptor families are called the same way one level down.
Rescue
A receptor in a community with too few characterized receptors, but within the novelty threshold of a known receptor family, takes its nearest ligand type in the embedding. 'Not rescued to' on a gene card names a ligand type that was refused and the reason. The reason is that the receptor lies outside the distance limit set by that ligand type's characterized receptors, that its residue at that ligand type's defining pocket position was measured and differs, or that its embedding neighborhood does not consistently support that ligand type.
Pocket evidence
The receptor's residues at the ligand-binding pocket positions, read from its alignment to the Pfam 7tm_1 model. When the whole pocket points to a ligand type that has a defining residue, that residue confirms the call if it is present. A different residue at a defining position vetoes a community call when the rest of the pocket also points elsewhere, and a gap at that position never counts against a ligand type.
Stability
How much a receptor's calls depend on which other receptors from the same species were classified with it. The classifier is rerun on random subsets of the species' receptors, and stability is the fraction of the runs including the receptor that give it the same call as the full run. It does not change the call, and receptors drawn into none of the subsets have no stability value.
Policy (admitted, rejected, unmeasured)
The isoform policy picks one protein per gene. A gene is admitted when any of its isoforms passes the class-A filter, and a passing isoform represents it. A gene with no passing isoform is unmeasured if some isoform lacks a measurement the filter needs, and rejected otherwise.
