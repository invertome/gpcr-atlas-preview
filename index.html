Molluscan GPCR atlas

Class-A G protein-coupled receptors of 577 species of Mollusca and 15 outgroup species, within Lophotrochozoa, arranged taxonomically.

The atlas lists the class-A GPCRs of 577 species in eight classes of Mollusca and 15 outgroup species from six other phyla. Proteins come from genome assemblies for 548 species (87 with published annotations, 461 with BRAKER gene predictions) and transcriptome assemblies for 44 species without a genome. Each receptor is one gene with a protein that carries class-A evidence and at least six predicted transmembrane helices. A protein language model (ESM-2 3B) turns each receptor into an embedding, and receptors are grouped with 6,708 reference receptors of known ligand type into communities. A receptor takes a ligand type when its ligand-binding pocket confirms it, or when most references in its community share it, the pocket does not contradict it, and, for a narrow majority, the nearest reference agrees. Receptor families follow the same rules one level down. Weak or conflicting evidence leaves only a ligand type, or none.

Of the 296,758 receptors, 33,025 (11.1%) have a named receptor family, 156,577 (52.8%) a ligand type only, and 107,156 (36.1%) no ligand type. Among those without a ligand type, 62,986 sit in communities with too few references and 44,170 did not pass the agreement rules. Separately, 62,927 receptors (21.2%) lie beyond the novelty threshold and are the candidate chemoreceptors, 54,464 of them in those communities.
