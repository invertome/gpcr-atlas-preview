Molluscan GPCR atlas

Class-A G protein-coupled receptors of 578 species of Mollusca and 15 outgroup species, within Lophotrochozoa, arranged by NCBI Taxonomy.

The atlas holds 296,758 class-A GPCRs, called in 592 of 593 species. For each receptor, the classifier tries to name a ligand type and, where the evidence allows, a receptor family. 33,025 receptors (11.1%) end with a named receptor family, 156,577 (52.8%) with a ligand type only, and 107,156 (36.1%) with no ligand type.

Ligand type
The first level of the classification, for example peptide, aminergic or opsin.
Receptor family
The level below, for example FMRFamide/myosuppressin within peptide.
