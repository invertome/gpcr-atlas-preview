Candidate chemoreceptors

62,927 receptors (21.2%) in 586 species are candidate chemoreceptors.

A receptor is a candidate chemoreceptor when it lies beyond the novelty threshold, farther from every characterized receptor family than all but 0.5% of characterized non-chemoreceptor receptors held out from the reference set. The tables give the ligand type call of the candidate chemoreceptors, because the set includes receptors with a named receptor family, a ligand type only and no ligand type.
